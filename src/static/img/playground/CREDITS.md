under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no attribution is required, it is kept here for
the record. The originals are the 1K JPG downloads, converted to WebP (the colour maps at quality 82, the height map
at quality 90) with `cwebp`. Nothing else was changed.
